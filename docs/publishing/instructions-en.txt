1. Install and power on to enter the aquarium. Play offline with no network setup. This version resets progress on restart; its clock starts from the firmware build time.
2. Use Up/Down to select a home action and tap OK to perform it. Hold OK on other screens to return to the tank.
3. Choose Feed to watch fish chase food. Choose Clean, use Up/Down to change rows, and press OK to wipe. In Choose Fish, pick a companion and its size, then confirm to add it.
4. Toggle Water Sound for background bubbles. After three minutes without input, the controls hide for immersive viewing. Press any key to restore the menu and continue.
